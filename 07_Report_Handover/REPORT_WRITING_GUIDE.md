Suggested wording for the results section:

> The optimised 1×2 array demonstrated higher radiation efficiency and better impedance matching than the basic 4×4 implementation under the simulated conditions. The larger array provided higher directivity and narrower principal-plane beams but achieved only a 0.40 dB increase in peak realized gain at 150 GHz. The increased full-structure loss fractions and stronger impedance mismatch explain that limited gain improvement. The contribution of individual feed junctions requires separate loss analysis.

Avoid claims of general array superiority, component-resolved loss attribution, full 4×4 optimisation or good 150 GHz matching. Identify which collaborator will cross-check the comparison, captions and native power definitions in the [tasks list](COLLABORATION_TASKS.md).
